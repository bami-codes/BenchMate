yes/no confirmation is a separate assertion, not a reference reassignment.

original pre-photo publication. Source and range-specific intervals, 9mV
waveform rules, meter allowances and forecast digest remain unchanged. This
adapter is frozen and published before requesting or accepting the numeric
reading. For R1 resistance, use USB off, wait 10 seconds, remove the indicated
resistor from the circuit and read its free numeric display on the 20kΩ range.
Record USB-off/isolation attestation, selected range, displayed resolution and
unit. Do not score an in-circuit reading as an isolated component measurement.

optional; without one, the original board photograph is context only and does
not authenticate the reported number. Opening the seal does not claim repair
or restored-diagnosis success; those remain separate physical stages.

The additive keyless auditor checks imported parent/child chains, exact raw

