# video/

`film/` is the demo film's pipeline: `shots.html` + `shots.js` compose every frame from `site/data` (the run records),
`render.js` renders it frame by frame, `audio.py` mixes the bed, the sound effects and the narration, and `voice/` holds the
narration clips with their word timings. `film/README.md` has the commands. `fonts/` are the typefaces the film and the site share.

The finished film: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFlJHOEapV8. Earlier drafts of the pipeline were removed from the tree
and remain in the history.
